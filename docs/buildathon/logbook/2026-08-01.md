## Decisions

1. **Job queue: Postgres `SKIP LOCKED`, not Redis.** Enqueueing an agent job and writing the state transition it follows from happen in one transaction. With a separate queue they cannot — a Storm File could change state while its follow-on job is silently lost, producing an orphaned claim with nothing in the ledger recording the failure. That is the exact outcome this platform exists to prevent, so we removed the possibility structurally rather than monitoring for it.

2. **Time to Relief redefined.** Was *verified claim → relief*, which started the clock after verification — the hard part, and the part a finance or audit audience will interrogate. Now *claim filed → first relief confirmed*. Measured against our replay this moves the median from 34h to 39h against a 72h target. We gave up five hours of headline for a number that survives the obvious question.

3. **Scope gap found and closed.** Payer routing, the insurer FNOL packet, and the donation → donor-journey path were all marked P0 in the PRD and appear as steps 4, 6 and 8 of our own acceptance test — but appeared nowhere in the build plan and had no owner. Found in a cross-document audit. All three now assigned across week 3 and marked not-cuttable; the insurer portal and portfolio heatmap stay out of scope at P1.

4. **Relief amounts:** flat J$45,000 cash grant per verified household, plus goods tiered by triage severity. Flat cash keeps any per-claim valuation judgement out of the agent path.

5. **A fictional insurer ("Island Mutual") in all demo UI.** Real carriers appear only in market sizing, so nothing on screen implies an endorsement we do not have.

6. **Privacy:** the pre-landfall anticipatory list is Director-role only. It is a ranked register of vulnerable people and where they live; publishing it would invert the privacy posture the rest of the platform argues for. Transparency obligations are met by aggregate-only public reporting.

## Findings and risks

**The WhatsApp 24-hour session window changes our demo run sheet.** Act 1 sends a free-form Patois alert cascade. A judge's phone has never messaged us, so at Act 1 it sits outside WhatsApp's 24-hour customer-service window, where only pre-approved templates may be sent — the alert would not deliver. The fix is sequencing, not engineering: the judge joins the sandbox *before Act 1* rather than before Act 2, which both enrols them and opens the window. This is the class of bug that works on every team phone and fails on the one phone that matters, so it gets tested well before rehearsal week.

**Highest-risk demo dependency: sandbox international delivery.** Twilio warns that sandbox delivery to international numbers is best-effort, and our users and demo are Jamaican numbers on a US sandbox number. Mitigations agreed: repeated testing from Jamaican numbers at varied times, Meta Cloud API business verification pursued in parallel, a pre-recorded voice-note fallback kept one keypress away, and a rehearsed threshold at which the presenter switches to it without commentary.

**Trial-account artefacts.** Outbound SMS currently arrives prefixed with "Sent from your Twilio trial account -", and trial accounts can only SMS pre-verified numbers. Both need clearing before demo day.
